HOW WE GOT HERE

The full story.

We started by running an Ahrefs content gap analysis — a tool that compares your keyword rankings against competitors and shows you queries where they rank but you don't. Against HeatPressNation and MakerFlo, we identified 218 keywords we're missing, representing an estimated 6,890 monthly searches. That's real opportunity.

But Ahrefs only tracks keywords in its own database — it typically undersells actual traffic by a significant margin. When we connected Google Search Console (GSC), which shows the actual data Google has on our site, two things became clear. First: we're already appearing for far more searches than Ahrefs estimated — 7.8 million impressions over six months. Second: we're converting almost none of them. Our site-wide click-through rate is 0.86%, against an industry benchmark of around 3%. We have pages ranking #1 getting 0.08% CTR. Pages at position 4 getting 0.04% CTR. That's not a ranking problem — that's a snippet problem.

Why CTR is low — the root cause

Title tags and meta descriptions are the text Google shows in search results. When a title doesn't contain the words someone searched for, when it's generic brand language ("The Stainless Depot Company"), or when the meta description is 1,800 characters that gets truncated mid-sentence — searchers scroll past even when we rank on page 1. Several pages have no meta description set at all, so Google auto-generates one from random page content. Fixing these snippets is faster and higher-ROI than building new content.

What the competitor analysis found

HeatPressNation and MakerFlo each rank for keywords we don't, but the pattern is consistent: they win with dedicated collection pages for each product category. "Wholesale tumblers" — collection page. "Bulk tumblers" — collection page. "Sublimation wood blanks" — collection page. They're not doing anything technically sophisticated. They just have a page for everything they sell.

Several of our gaps are simply missing collection pages for products we already carry, or collection pages that are competing against each other for the same queries. The competitor keywords we can realistically capture: wholesale tumblers, bulk tumblers, sublimation tumblers, sublimation blanks, Sawgrass sublimation printers, sublimation wood blanks. Keywords we're deliberately not chasing: anything Epson-specific (we don't carry Epson), heat press terms (sunsetting that line), and luggage tags (don't sell them).

PRIORITY 1 · SNIPPET REWRITES

10 pages. Update all snippets by EOD today. Then monitor individually.

These are the 10 pages with the largest gap between how many times Google is showing them in search results and how many people are clicking. Each page was selected by calculating total recoverable clicks — a page at position 3 with 100K impressions and 0.1% CTR is losing more clicks than a page at position 8 with 10K impressions and 0.5% CTR, even though the second has worse CTR. We sorted by total opportunity, not just percentage.

How to implement — same steps for all 10 pages

For collection pages: Shopify admin → Collections → click the collection → scroll to bottom → "Search engine listing" → "Edit website SEO" → paste proposed title and meta → Save.

For blog posts: Shopify admin → Blog posts → click the post → same "Search engine listing" section at bottom.

For product pages: Shopify admin → Products → click the product → same section.

After saving: Search for each page in an incognito browser 3–5 days later to confirm the new snippet shows. If Google still shows old text, it hasn't re-crawled yet — give it up to 2 weeks. If it shows something completely different from what you set, Google is overriding your title (see task notes for why).

Monitoring: Once all 10 are deployed, pull the GSC queries-by-page report for each individually each week. You're watching for CTR improvement — not just position. These 10 are being monitored separately before rolling out the same approach to the remaining 242 low-CTR pages.

PRIORITY 2 · COLLECTION PAGE ROLES

Six pages. Each one now owns a specific audience and non-overlapping keywords.

The core problem: multiple collection pages were all appearing for the same search queries at the same time. When Google sees several pages from the same site targeting the same query, it picks one and suppresses the rest. Neither ranks as well as a single dedicated page would. This is called keyword cannibalization and it's been limiting our ranking potential on some of the most valuable queries in our category.

The new architecture — six pages, six distinct audiences
  • /drinkware — all tumbler types, individual units, no minimums. For crafters, makers, one-offs. Keywords: "stainless steel tumbler," "custom tumblers"
  • /shop-by-the-case — all tumbler types, case quantities. For resellers, shops, B2B. Keywords: "wholesale tumblers," "bulk tumblers," "tumblers in bulk" — queries HPN ranks #1 for and we currently don't appear for
  • /sublimitable — everything sublimatable, all quantities. Sublimation department hub. Keywords: "sublimation tumblers," "sublimation blanks," "sublimation cups"
  • /powdercoated-cups — powder coated tumblers by the unit. For DTC buyers + laser engraving/DTF one-offs. Keywords: "tumblers" (222K impressions), "powder coated tumblers"
  • /powdercoated-cases — powder coated tumblers in bulk. For laser shops and DTF businesses buying in volume. Keywords: "powder coated tumblers wholesale," "bulk powder coated tumblers"
  • /powder-coated — parent hub for all powder coated products (all quantities). Mirrors /sublimitable structure.

PRIORITY 5 · BACKLOG

Shopify Markets hreflang bug — diagnosed, deprioritized.

During the GSC analysis we found a Shopify Markets configuration issue causing French and Spanish blog URLs to appear in search results for global audiences who can't read them. One French blog post URL got 565,000 impressions over six months — with 0.07% coming from France, and 25% from Brazil. The root cause is confirmed. The revenue impact is minimal: the affected traffic was never going to convert because Brazilian, Indian, and US searchers can't read a French-language page. The fix requires IT and has potential commerce side effects. Backlog item until capacity opens up.

Root cause and fix

Problem: Shopify Markets generates hreflang tags as "fr-US" and "es-US" because the global French and Spanish markets are configured with geographic targeting set to "United States." Google can't interpret these correctly and serves the French/Spanish URLs globally.

Fix: Shopify admin → Settings → Markets → change geographic targeting for global French and Spanish markets from "United States" to "Rest of world." This changes the auto-generated hreflang tags to the correct "fr" and "es" format.

Caution: Markets settings also control currency, payment methods, shipping zones, and pricing. Don't change geographic targeting in isolation — review full market settings with IT before saving. Recovery time: 4–12 weeks after the fix.

Hard keywords (KD 50+). Every keyword in this plan is KD 10 or below. The opportunity is in uncontested queries where a good page wins without a campaign.
